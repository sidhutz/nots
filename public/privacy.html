Your student account.
Your private notes.

This app uses Google to sign you in safely. It does not ask for or store your Google password.

What gets saved

  • Your Google account identifier, Google email, Google display name, and profile photo URL.
  • Your student profile form: full name, contact email, and fixed role Student.
  • Your uploaded note files, their file names, size, type, and upload time.
  • A hashed session token and its expiry, so you can stay signed in securely.
  • Your personal text notes and to-do list items.
  • Public study posts and optional attachments with their likes, comments, and reposts. These are visible to other signed-in students; your personal notes and private uploads stay private.
  • Task due dates are saved with your to-do items. Browser reminders are shown only while the dashboard is open, after you enable notifications.
  • An unfinished note draft is temporarily saved in this browser's local storage so it can be recovered on this device.
  • Activity events such as sign-in/out, dashboard views, and profile or note updates. Event details do not copy note or to-do content.

Who can see the notes

Your private uploaded files and personal written notes are tied to your signed-in account. Public study posts and attached photos or files are visible to signed-in students, who can like, comment, and repost them. You can see who liked or reposted each post.

What does not get saved

Google passwords, Gmail messages, Drive files, access tokens, and refresh tokens are not stored in the database. Access tokens are used briefly on the server to fetch the Google profile during login.

Where data is stored

Account and student data are stored in the site owner’s Supabase database. Uploaded note files are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket. The website server controls access and users can only access their own records.

Your account controls

From the dashboard you can download a JSON copy of your account data or request permanent account deletion. Deletion removes your profile, public posts and their interactions, private notes, uploaded files, tasks, and active sessions.

For the site owner

Before a public launch, add your organization details, a real privacy contact, file-retention rules, deletion workflow, and backup policy.
